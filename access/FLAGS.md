# Flags

`/lantern offer` is the step that needs typing a sentence. The player types the draft after `offer`. Offer stays. It is in the frozen command list and the frozen event schema.

These are one confirm:

- `/lantern join`
- `/lantern stop`
- `/lantern yes`
- the mark stick
- the confirm lantern
- the stall lantern

`/embers`, `/embers light`, and `/embers dark` are one confirm each.
